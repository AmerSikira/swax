## Coverage boundaries

- External adapters use deterministic fixtures. Live Google consent, real analytics freshness/quotas, paid AI responses, provider billing, and production infrastructure need separate integration validation.
- Synchronous queue execution does not establish actual worker progress, scheduled execution, retry coordination, or tenant-context reset. Use the worker-backed profile for those claims.
- Chromium is the initial profile. Additional browsers and sustained concurrent-load checks are later test-expansion decisions.
- Fake provider output matching a contract does not prove the behavior of every live model; focused adapter contracts and supported-model validation complement browser journeys.
- Financial packages, invoice PDFs, subscription purchasing, and other Fakturax billing controls are outside Swax's initial product scope.
- Numerical classification, credential failures, owner replacement, and granular permissions stay policy-dependent. A fixture must not silently decide an unresolved rule.
